How to read it

  • Brands captured: an independent AI reader read about 6,300 live menus. Of the brands it found, the share our data also lists. This is the main measure.
  • Human check: the same question, answered by people reading 514 menus by hand. Smaller sample, most trustworthy.
  • Listings confirmed: of the brands we list at those outlets, the share the AI reader also found. The reader misses things too, so this is a minimum: a low number means "check it", not "wrong".
Brands are counted when a bar or restaurant’s online drink menu lists them, September 2026, US and Canada. The check covers 225 major brands, not full portfolios. Brands are grouped by their current owner.
