How to read it

  • Brands captured (reader agreement): of the brand listings the independent reader found on those menus, the share our board also lists (n = listings the reader found).
  • Listings confirmed: of the listings our board has at the checked outlets, the share the reader also found (n = our listings checked). The reader misses things too, so this is a minimum: a low number means "check it", not "wrong".
  • Outlets (checked brands only): outlets on the board that list at least one of the manufacturer's checked brands, not its whole portfolio.
  • 95% interval: Wilson score interval for the rate at that n. Small n means a wide interval; read those rows as a guide, not a verdict.
  • Pass / Watch rule: Pass when brands captured is 95% or more AND listings confirmed is 85% or more. Otherwise Watch.
  • As quoted for October at certification: the figures published when the October board was certified, measured on the previous board with their own samples; each says what it measures.
October 2026. Brands are counted when a bar or restaurant’s online drink menu lists them, US and Canada. Outlets = outlets on the October 2026 board listing at least one of the manufacturer's checked brands, out of 105,542 branded outlets. The check covers 225 major brands, not full portfolios.
