How to read it

  • Brands we list that are on the menu: of the brands we list at a random sample of 10,000 outlets, the share an independent check found on the outlet’s menu.
  • Brands we say are absent that are absent: of the 60 most-listed brands, where we say an outlet does not list one, the share the independent check also did not find.
  • Listings confirmed: of the listings our board has at the checked outlets, the share the reader also found (n = our listings checked). The reader misses things too, so this is a minimum: a low number means "check it", not "wrong".
  • Outlets (checked brands only): outlets on the board that list at least one of the manufacturer's checked brands, not its whole portfolio.
  • 95% interval: Wilson score interval for the rate at that n. Small n means a wide interval; read those rows as a guide, not a verdict.
  • Live-menu check: people-checked reading of the live websites of a random sample of about 10,000 outlets in October 2026, compared with our certified October board brand by brand. Precision counts our listings the live site confirms; recall counts the brands on the pages we collected that we also list. Brands on pages we did not collect are not in this recall figure.
November 2026. Brands are counted when a bar or restaurant’s online drink menu lists them, US and Canada. Outlets = outlets on the November 2026 board listing at least one of the manufacturer's checked brands, out of 97,210 branded outlets. The check covers 405 major brands, not full portfolios. Brands are grouped by their current owner; Cîroc is shown under Main Street Advisors, the Diageo–MSA joint venture. People-read checks were not repeated for this month.
