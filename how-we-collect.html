How we collect · Privacy

What we read, how we read it, and what we do with your details.

Plain answers for procurement, legal and anyone curious.

Where the data comes from

  • Drink menus that bars and restaurants publish on the public web, read as any visitor would see them.
  • No logins, paywalls or private systems.
  • Outlet names and addresses come from public business listings and public licence records.
  • We don't collect personal data about venue staff or guests.

Venues: corrections and removal

If you run a venue and something we show is wrong, or you'd rather we didn't read your menu, email ed@dobbles.ai and we'll correct or remove it.

Brand names

Brand and company names belong to their owners. Showing a brand doesn't imply any affiliation with, or endorsement by, its owner or the venue.

Privacy

What we collect from you

Only what you choose to send us. Our forms open an email from your own mail app with the details you typed: brand, work email, role, and for walkthroughs your name, company and question. Clients who sign in use an email and password; the scorecard is decrypted in your browser.

How we use it

  • To send what you asked for, and to follow up once.
  • We don't sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. Signed-in clients' sessions are stored in their own browser.

Seeing or deleting your details

Email ed@dobbles.ai to see or delete what we hold about you.
