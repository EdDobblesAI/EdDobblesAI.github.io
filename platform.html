How it works

One library of bar and restaurant menus. Read every month. Checked before it ships.

We capture each menu and keep it, dated. Everything you see — presence, competitors, gaps, cocktails — is read from that library and traceable back to the page.

From a menu page to your scorecard

Six steps, every month. A month reaches clients only after the last one.

  1. 01
    Find
    On-premise outlets across the US and Canada, refreshed monthly.
  2. 02
    Capture
    The outlet's own drink menu pages, as a visitor sees them.
  3. 03
    Read
    Every alcohol listing pulled out, with its cocktail where shown.
  4. 04
    Match
    Spellings combined; each brand tied to its current owner.
  5. 05
    Verify
    Each brand confirmed in the page text; closed, duplicate and retail outlets removed.
  6. 06
    Certify
    Re-checks pass, the month is signed off and published.

How we count

Every headline number comes with its definition.

From the September 2026 certified board.

Bars and restaurants with a drink menu online122,587

Outlets whose own drink menu we read that month, or that list at least one brand. Closed, duplicate, retail (liquor store) and cannabis outlets are removed. Includes 832 chain locations without their own page, shown with their chain's core drink list and marked as inferred.

Menus that name at least one brand106,468

Of the outlets above, those whose menu names at least one alcohol brand. A brewery or winery listing only its own products doesn't count. This is the base for every share-of-menu figure.

Brands per branded menu22 median

Distinct brands on each branded menu. Cocktail names don't count as brands.

How you see it

Scorecard

Top-tier restaurants

Your presence on the menus of critically recognised restaurants.

What we measure

  • Whether a brand is on an outlet's published drink menu
  • Where it sits on the menu: cocktail list, by the glass or bottle
  • Change month over month, on certified months only

What we don't

  • Sales volume, depletions or market share
  • Outlets with no drink menu online
  • Back-bar or well pours a menu doesn't list
