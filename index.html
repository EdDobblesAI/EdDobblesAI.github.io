How it works

We collect the menus

Every month we collect the drink menus that bars and restaurants publish on their own websites.

We read every brand

Each listing is read and matched to a brand and its current owner. Spelling variants are combined, and brands we can't confirm are left out.

We check the work

Results are checked against independent re-reads of thousands of menus and against menus read by people, brand by brand.

What you see

Share of menus. How many outlets list your brands, and what share of all alcohol-serving outlets that is.
Competitors. Your rank against other companies, and each brand against its category.
By state. Where your brands are strong and where they are thin.
Menus without you. Outlets that serve alcohol but carry none of your brands: the prospect list.
