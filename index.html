The on-premise problem

Brands are built on-premise. It's also the channel you can see least.

Depletions stop at the distributor. Field audits cover the doors your team can visit. Between them, most of what happens on the menu goes unseen until it shows up in the numbers.

"Our distributor says we're in. Are we on the menu?"

Being stocked isn't being listed. A brand that's behind the bar but not on the menu is easy for a guest to pass over.

"Our reps can't be everywhere."

Without a target list, reps revisit accounts they already have. The bars that pour your category but not your brand stay unvisited.

"We hear about competitor wins too late."

A rival takes the signature cocktail in a key market. You find out a quarter later, when the share report moves.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers

Is this sales data?

No. It's whether a brand is listed on an outlet's published drink menu. Pair it with depletions for volume.

What about outlets with no menu online?

They aren't in our count. We say how many outlets we cover and how we define them — see How we count.

How accurate is it?

We publish capture rates by manufacturer, checked by a separate AI reader and by people reading 514 menus by hand. See the accuracy page.

Where does the data come from?

Only drink menus that venues publish on the public web. No logins, no paywalls, no personal data. How we collect.

Can I see coverage for my states and channels?

Yes — in the walkthrough, cut for your markets. We'll publish a split here once it's certified.

What does it cost?

Pilots are scoped to your portfolio and markets. We share pricing on the walkthrough call.
