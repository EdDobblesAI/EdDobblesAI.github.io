Use cases

What on-premise teams do with menu data.

Each use starts with a question your team already asks — and ends with a list they can act on.

Sales & distributionBrand teamsStrategy & insightsInnovation & craftTrade marketing
Sales & distribution

Send reps to the doors worth winning.

The question: which bars should my team walk into next?

  • Accounts in our coverage that list your category but none of your brands
  • What they pour instead, so the rep walks in knowing the competition
  • Filter by state, city, chain or independent
What you get

A target list your sales leaders can split by territory — each account with the brands it lists today and a link to its menu.

Brand teams

Know where you're strong — and where you're thin.

The question: how many menus carry us, and is that growing?

  • Menus carrying each brand, and your share of branded menus
  • Rank against your category, nationally and state by state
  • Month-over-month change on certified months
What you get

A scorecard for your company and every brand in it, ready for a brand review or a distributor meeting.

Strategy & insights

Catch competitor wins before the share report does.

The question: where is a rival winning that we aren't?

  • Head to head: outlets carrying them, you, both or neither
  • Your company's rank against peer drinks companies
  • Portfolio depth: how many of your brands each menu carries
What you get

A competitive-gaps view by outlet, so strategy and sales are looking at the same list.

Innovation & craft

Spot the small brands breaking through.

The question: which emerging brands are gaining menus, and where?

  • Emerging brands by menu count and month-over-month gain
  • The cities and venue types adopting them first
  • Partnership and acquisition screening from the menu up
What you get

A watch list of rising brands in your categories, with the menus they're appearing on.

Trade marketing

Confirm the cocktails you support are live.

The question: are the cocktails and activations we back actually on the menu?

  • Cocktails that name your brand, outlet by outlet
  • Accounts in your programs checked against their published menu each month
  • The menu line and its date, to share internally
What you get

A monthly check of which menus call your brand out by name, with the evidence behind each one.
